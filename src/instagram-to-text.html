Instagram to Text

Supports Instagram video and Reels links. Transcription runs on the server — your link and results are never stored on this page.

The tool above does one thing: paste any public Instagram video or Reel link, and it returns everything that was said as plain text you can copy or download.

How to convert Instagram to text

  1. Copy the link of the Instagram video or Reel (the share link looks like instagram.com/reel/...).
  2. Paste it into the box above and press Transcribe.
  3. Wait a moment — the spoken content appears as text.
  4. Copy it, or download it as a TXT file or SRT subtitles.

What is an Instagram transcript?

An Instagram transcript is the written record of what was said in a video — not the caption the creator typed underneath it. Instagram itself never shows the spoken words as selectable text: they live inside the audio (or as burned-in captions that can't be copied). This page produces that written record for you, with every line kept in the order it was spoken, so you can quote, search, or repurpose it.

Instagram Transcript Generator

This page is a generator in the practical sense: you paste a link, it does the listening and typing for you, and hands back an editable result. There is no account to create and nothing to install — the whole job runs in your browser and on our server, and the text is yours to copy or download the moment it appears. If a video has no captions at all, the speech is still picked up from the audio, so captionless Reels work too.

Instagram Reels Transcript

Reels get their own note because they are what most people paste here. A Reel's share link (instagram.com/reel/...) is all the tool needs — Reels don't require a separate download or screen recording. The transcript keeps the timing of the original audio, so a line you copy still corresponds to the moment it was said, which makes SRT subtitles straightforward if you need them.

Who uses this

  • People who want to quote a video accurately instead of paraphrasing from memory.
  • Non-native speakers who find text easier to follow than fast speech.
  • Anyone taking notes from long Reels without rewatching them.

FAQ

How do I transcribe an Instagram video?
Copy the video's share link, paste it into the box at the top of this page, and press Transcribe. The spoken content comes back as text in a few seconds — longer videos with no existing captions can take a little longer, since the speech has to be generated from the audio. You can then copy the text or export it as TXT or SRT.
Does it work with private accounts?
No. Only public content can be transcribed.
Which languages are supported?
The transcription service detects the spoken language automatically and returns what was said.
Do I need an account?
No signup, no email — paste a link and go.
