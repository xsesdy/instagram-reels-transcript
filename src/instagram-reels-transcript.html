Getting a transcript from a Reel

  1. Open the Reel in the Instagram app, tap the share icon, copy the link.
  2. Paste it in the tool at the top of this page.
  3. Press Transcribe and give it a moment.
  4. Read, copy, or download the transcript (TXT or SRT).

Instagram Reels to Text

That is the whole idea of this page — Instagram Reels to text, with no detours. The transcript is produced on the server side and shown only to you; when you close the page it is gone.

Why a transcript beats rewatching

  • Grab quotable lines with the SRT file keeping their timing.

FAQ

My link starts with instagram.com/reel/ — fine?
Yes, and /reels/ links are fine too. Both are the same kind of content.
The Reel has background music and talking — what do I get?
The transcript covers the spoken words; pure music sections produce nothing to write down.
