igscript.com

About

igscript.com is a free, no-frills tool that turns Instagram videos and Reels into text. Paste a link, get a transcript, and copy it or download it as TXT or SRT subtitles.

The site is built and maintained by Xsesdy. It exists for one reason: watching a good video shouldn't stop you from quoting, translating, or taking notes on what was actually said.

How it works: when you submit a link, the URL is passed to a third-party transcription service on the server side. Your link and the transcript are never stored — everything lives in your browser session and disappears when you close the page.
