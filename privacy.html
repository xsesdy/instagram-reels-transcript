Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

What we process

When you submit an Instagram link, we send that URL to our third-party transcription provider (Supadata) to retrieve the video's transcript. We do not store your link, the transcript, or any personal data on our servers. Transcripts exist only in your browser session and disappear when you close the page.

To enforce the free daily quota, we keep a per-visitor counter keyed on a one-way hash of your IP address and the current date. This counter contains nothing but two numbers (today's uses and credits), it cannot be linked back to you or to the links you submit, and it is automatically deleted within 48 hours.

Third-party services

  • Supadata (supadata.ai) — transcription provider: receives the Instagram URL you submit and fetches the video's speech in order to return a transcript. The link is used only to produce that transcript.
  • Cloudflare — hosting and content delivery: serves this website and processes standard request data (such as IP address, user agent and requested URL) for security, caching and performance.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: uses a JavaScript beacon and the browser Performance API to measure aggregate page views, visitor activity and page-performance metrics. Cloudflare states that its Web Analytics service does not collect or use visitors’ personal data.
  • PayPal — payments: if you buy a Pro pass, the payment itself is handled by PayPal under its own privacy policy. We only receive a payment confirmation; we never see your card details.

We do not use advertising networks or social-media trackers.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics uses a JavaScript beacon for the aggregate metrics described above. The transcription tool does not set tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set strictly functional cookies that are required for security and content delivery; those cookies are not used to profile you.

YouTube API Services

Our operator-controlled publishing tool uses YouTube API Services solely to upload videos to the YouTube channel authorized by the account holder and to set the selected video’s title, description and visibility setting. It requests only the permission needed for that upload function.

The tool stores an authorization token locally on the operator-controlled computer. It does not use the token to read unrelated account information, sell or share YouTube API data, or upload to another channel without authorization. Our use of information received from YouTube API Services is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking YouTube authorization and deletion requests

You may revoke this tool’s access to your Google Account at any time through Google’s Security settings. You may also request revocation by emailing contact@igscript.com with the subject “Revoke YouTube authorization.” For a request made through this mechanism, we revoke the authorization token promptly and delete Authorized Data obtained under that consent as soon as possible, and no later than 7 calendar days after revocation.

To request deletion of personal data held by this service, email contact@igscript.com with the subject “Data deletion request.” We will confirm the request, identify the relevant data and delete it in accordance with applicable law and the stated retention requirements for the relevant service.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site evolves. The date above reflects the latest revision.
