Fill it in from top to bottom

  1. Save the source first. Keep the Reel link and the day you checked it so you can return to the same context.
  2. Write the spoken line before summarising. Put an important claim in the spoken field and add a timestamp only when you need to locate it again.
  3. Check the frame separately. A product, title card, or gesture may add context, but it is not proof that the speaker said it.
  4. Add one action in your own words. Verify a claim, try a technique, or record a question. Label it as the follow-up rather than blending it into the source.
The useful boundary: a transcription can be accurate about the audio while still missing a visual step. The template keeps that limitation visible instead of hiding it in a polished summary.

When a simple transcript is enough

Use plain text when you only need a short quote, a rough outline, or a phrase to search later. Use the review template when a Reel has a claim that you may rely on, a tutorial step shown on screen, or an instruction you want to test. The extra fields are not busywork; they make it clear what came from the source and what came from you.

For a public Reel with clear speech, use Instagram to Text to create the copyable transcript before filling in the template.

FAQ

Can I use this template for a Reel with no spoken audio?
Yes, but leave the spoken-line field empty and record that the meaning comes from the frame. Do not invent dialogue.
Should I copy every word from the Reel?
No. Keep the full transcript separately when needed, then use this template for the line or moment that requires a careful check.
Why include a next-action field?
It prevents your own interpretation, task, or question from being confused with what the creator actually said or showed.
