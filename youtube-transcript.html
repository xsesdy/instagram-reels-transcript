YouTube Transcript

TXT is plain text without timestamps · SRT keeps the timing of every line.
No signup, no video upload. Transcription runs on the server — your link and the result are never stored.

The tool above does one thing: paste a public YouTube link, and it returns everything that was said as text you can read, copy, or download — each line stamped with the moment it was spoken.

How to get a YouTube transcript

  1. Copy the link of the YouTube video (the address looks like youtube.com/watch?v=...).
  2. Paste it into the box above and press Transcribe.
  3. Wait a few seconds — the spoken content appears with timestamps.
  4. Copy it, or download it as TXT or SRT.

YouTube Transcript Generator

This page is a generator in the practical sense: paste a link, and the listening and typing are done for you. YouTube videos usually have a caption track already — creators upload their own, or YouTube generates one automatically — and in both cases the existing track is fetched directly, which is the fastest path. If a video has no captions at all, the speech is generated from the audio instead, which takes a little longer but still returns the full text. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

YouTube Video Transcription

Manual captions and auto-generated captions are two different things, and it helps to know which you are getting. Manual captions are typed (or carefully edited) by the creator, so they are the most accurate text a video can offer. Auto-generated captions are produced by YouTube's own speech recognition for most uploads — good on clear single-speaker audio, weaker on accents, crosstalk, and heavy background music, and usually light on punctuation. This page fetches whichever track exists, preferring the manual one. The transcript comes back in the language that was spoken in the video — it is not translated.

YouTube Video to Text

YouTube shows a transcript panel inside the player, but it never lets you export it: there is no download button, and copying gives you a formatting mess with no timing. Turning a YouTube video to text means getting the spoken content as a file you keep — text you can quote, search, paste into notes, or line up with the video as SRT subtitles. That is exactly what this page hands back.

Who uses this

FAQ

Is this a YouTube video transcriber?
Yes — paste a public video link and the spoken content comes back as text. Videos with a caption track return in seconds; captionless videos are generated from the audio and take a little longer.
What if the video has no captions?
The words are picked up from the audio itself, so captionless videos still work — those take a little longer to process.
Which languages are supported?
The transcript is returned in the language spoken in the video. Translation is not offered.
Do I need an account?
No signup, no email — paste a link and go.
