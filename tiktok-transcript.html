TikTok Transcript

TXT is plain text without timestamps · SRT keeps the timing of every line.
No signup, no video upload. Transcription runs on the server — your link and the result are never stored.

The tool above does one thing: paste a public TikTok link, and it returns everything that was said as text you can read, copy, or download — each line stamped with the moment it was spoken.

How to get a TikTok transcript

  1. Tap the Share button on the TikTok video and copy the link.
  2. Paste it into the box above and press Transcribe.
  3. Wait a few seconds — the spoken content appears with timestamps.
  4. Copy it, or download it as TXT or SRT.

TikTok Transcript Generator

This page is a generator in the practical sense: paste a link, and the listening and typing are done for you. If the video has TikTok's own captions, the caption track is fetched directly — that is the fastest path. If the video has no captions at all, the speech is generated from the audio instead, which takes a little longer but still returns the full text. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

TikTok to Text

TikTok never lets you copy the spoken words of a video — auto-captions live inside the app as an overlay, and videos whose creators turned captions off show nothing at all. Turning TikTok to text means producing the written record yourself: what this tool hands back is the full spoken content in order, searchable, quotable, and ready to paste anywhere.

Who uses this

  • Creators who want to study or reuse the script of a viral video.
  • Researchers and journalists quoting a video accurately.
  • Anyone who prefers reading a clip to watching it twice.

FAQ

Does it work on videos with no captions?
Yes. When a video has no caption track, the words are picked up from the audio itself, so captionless TikToks work too — those take a little longer to process.
Can I download TikTok subtitles as SRT?
Yes. After transcribing, press Download SRT — every line keeps the timing of the original audio, so the file lines up with the video.
Does it work with private videos?
No. Only public TikTok videos can be transcribed.
Do I need an account?
No signup, no email — paste a link and go.
